    Hi there and Welcome!
    I’m a passionate 2D visual artist looking for opportunities to work in storyboard but also open to custom projects such as whiteboard animations, animatics, illustrations, and caricatures. I have extensive experience in storyboarding, animation, drawing and caricature, illustration, photography, fine art, managing individual projects from start to finish. I’m well-versed in Adobe Suite, Toon Boom Storyboard, TV Paint, Procreate. I’m continuously updating my skill set to include relevant and novel methods to provide high-end quality products. I believe in the power of telling stories through images. The storyboard is the environment where I feel comfortable expressing ideas and emotions in a simple, fast and efficient manner. My professional goal is to work in a creative environment that offers new challenges every day and where I can use my skills to contribute to and create unique, impactful, beautiful and creative projects. You can check my portfolio: https://cseniamorgun.wixsite.com/xeniamorgun. Please feel free to contact me here or via e-mail cseniamorgun@yahoo.be. I speak fluently in French, English, Romanian and Russian.
